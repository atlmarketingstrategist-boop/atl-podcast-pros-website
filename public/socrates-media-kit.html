About Socrates Charos

Bishop Socrates Charos is not your typical faith guest. Before becoming a healing minister, he was a champion ballroom dancer and the owner of Clearwater, Florida's historic Capitol Theatre, a man equally at home in the arts as in the miraculous. Then everything changed.

Today, Socrates is the founder of Church From All Nations and the host of Good News with Socrates, a weekly podcast and YouTube channel reaching believers across America, Greece, and Israel. His ministry carries extraordinary claims: 16 people raised from the dead, 23 brought out of comatose states, and over 70 documented cancer healings, all delivered with the unhurried confidence of a man who has seen things most people can only imagine.

He is also the man who discipled Angela Halili, co-founder of Girls Gone Bible, the number one religion and spirituality podcast on Spotify with over 20 million listens. He appeared on their show in September 2024, introducing himself to one of the largest faith audiences in digital media.

A Greek-American minister, storyteller, and supernatural eyewitness. Your audience has never heard a guest quite like Socrates Charos.
